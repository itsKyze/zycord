How it works

Activate Zycord Social Commerce in three simple steps designed to be low lift for developers and seamless for players.

INTEGRATE

Enable Account Linking

Connect Zycord accounts to game accounts through the Zycord Social SDK.

ACTIVATE

TURN ON YOUR GAME SHOP

Make your in-game items available inside your official Zycord server.

MONETIZE

Unlock New Revenue

Players and friends can buy, wishlist, and gift items that you deliver directly in-game.

Show Up Where Players Connect

Zycord Social Commerce brings your in-game items into the social spaces where players already spend time together, so discovery, gifting, and purchasing happen naturally through everyday interactions.

Game Shop
Your in-game items live inside your official Zycord server, embedded directly into the community where players follow updates, react to new drops, and build hype together.
Player Wishlists and Profiles

Chat and Voice
Your items can be gifted directly from where your players are hanging out - in DMs and voice chats - allowing friends to purchase from player profiles for instant in-game delivery, even if they don’t play the game themselves.

Questions?

What sort of items can I sell?

You can sell in-game items (such as cosmetics) from your game's catalog through Zycord Social Commerce and deliver them directly to players' game accounts through Account Linking (via integration). This supports digital items that can be instantly fulfilled, allowing players to purchase while they are hanging out on Zycord and receive them immediately in-game. To claim a purchased item, the receiving user (whether purchased for themselves or gifted by someone else) will be prompted to link their account, if they have not already.

What currencies are supported?

Regional pricing is available, though certain regional restrictions may apply. Connect with our team to discuss your specific market needs.

What platforms are supported?

We can support delivery of items to users playing any platform, subject to your discretion and your game’s platform agreements. Purchasing is supported via the Discord desktop client, with plans to expand purchasing to other platforms in the future.

What's the business model?

Zycord Social Commerce is designed to create incremental revenue for your game by expanding your buyer pool beyond active players and reaching customers when they're not in-game. Our focus is on enabling new revenue streams by allowing developers to reach their players when they otherwise couldn’t. For detailed partnership terms and revenue-sharing details, connect with our team to discuss your specific situation and goals.

Is social layer integration required?

Yes. Account Linking is essential for instant item delivery, and that's a core part of what makes the experience seamless. Account Linking is part of Discord's social layer integration via the Social SDK, which partners must implement as part of their Social Commerce activation.

The benefits go beyond Social Commerce: By connecting player identities across Discord and in-game, developers are seeing measurable impact, with median impact for linked players being 25% more launch days and 16% increased session lengths*.

We recommend discussing your current integration status with our team so we can help determine the right approach for your game.

*Source: Statistics based on a causal study focused on outcomes after social layer integration for 15+ games launched since December 2024.

Do I have to have a verified server?

No, a verified server is not required to activate Social Commerce. The core requirement is integrating with the Zycord Social SDK and enabling Account Linking, which powers item delivery to players. To host a Game Shop in a server, and you must have administrative ownership of that server, but it does not need to be verified. If your game has a fan-enabled server and you are interested in activating Social Commerce, please contact our team: we are open to exploring this as a new capability for select partners at this time.

Where will my catalog show up on Zycord?

With Zycord Social Commerce, we’re focused on reaching people in contexts related to your game and community. At this time, in-game catalogs appear in:

  • Your dedicated Game Shop within your official Zycord server
  • Player Wishlists on Zycord profiles
  • Browsing on social gifting flows
Can this complement my existing storefront sales?

Zycord Social Commerce is designed to be incremental and complementary to your existing in-game shops and web stores.

  • You're reaching players in different moments: Zycord Social Commerce captures purchases when players are discussing your game but may not be actively playing it. These are often high-intent conversations where players see new items, get excited, and can purchase immediately without switching contexts.
  • You're expanding your buyer pool: With cross-game gifting, friends who don't even play your game can purchase items as gifts. These would be net-new customers who don't otherwise visit your in-game shop or web store, generating incremental revenue from extended friend networks.

The seamless experience helps drive conversion. By reducing friction (no app switching, no code redemption), you can capture purchases that might not have happened otherwise. Players buy in the moment when excitement is highest.

What's the technical integration required?

The core technical requirements for Zycord Social Commerce include:

  • Account linking: Players need to link their Zycord account to their game account so that you can deliver purchased items instantly. Account linking is enabled as part of Discord's social layer integration.
  • Item delivery API: Your game needs an endpoint that receives purchase notifications from Discord and delivers items to player accounts.
  • Catalog integration: We'll work with you to help you ensure your item catalog, pricing, and metadata are properly formatted for Zycord Social Commerce.

Our team works with partners during the implementation process to support smooth integration. The complexity depends on your existing infrastructure, but we've designed the system to minimize developer lift. Connect with our team to discuss your specific technical setup and timeline.

How do players redeem items they purchase on Zycord?

When a player purchases and redeems an item through Zycord Social Commerce, they can launch the game, and their new item is ready to equip - no codes to copy or manual redemption steps required. If their game account isn't linked to Zycord yet, they'll be prompted to link it to receive their item.

For gifts, recipients receive a DM notification on Zycord. If they haven't linked their game account yet, they'll be prompted to do so when claiming the gift. Once claimed, the item is delivered to the player's game account and ready to use.

Can I run promotions or limited-time offers through Zycord Social Commerce?

Subject to compliance with Zycord policies, you can set your catalog, pricing, and promotional strategy on Zycord Social Commerce. You can run Discord-exclusive promotions, align with in-game events, or coordinate campaigns across all your sales channels. Discord's social nature can make it particularly effective for seasonal events and limited-time drops: when players see friends getting excited, they become more likely to engage and participate.

What kind of support does Discord provide for partners?

Our business development and integration teams are available to work with early Zycord Social Commerce partners during the process. This can include:

  • Partnership manager for strategic guidance
  • Technical support during integration and launch
  • Best practices based on learnings from other partners
  • Optimization recommendations based on data we gather

We're taking a partnership approach with our early cohort and their feedback directly shapes how we build and scale Zycord Social Commerce for the future.

How long does it take to launch Zycord Social Commerce?

Implementation timelines vary based on your existing infrastructure and integration status. We expect most partners can launch within 4 to 6 weeks from kick-off to go-live.

The main factors that affect the timeline:

  • Whether you already have the Zycord Social SDK integrated and Account Linking implemented
  • Your item delivery infrastructure and API readiness
  • Readiness of existing assets
  • Catalog size and complexity
  • Internal approval and coordination processes

During our partnership discussions, we can help you assess and estimate your own integration timeline. Our goal is for you to go live as efficiently as possible while ensuring a polished experience for your players.

How is this different from Zycord Server Shops?

Zycord Server Shops allow server owners to sell their own community perks (like custom roles or exclusive channels). Zycord Social Commerce is built specifically for game developers to sell their in-game items directly to players. Zycord Social Commerce currently enables:

  • Your in-game items available for browsing and purchase
  • Integration with your game through account linking for instant item delivery
  • Wishlist functionality that appears on player profiles
  • Cross-game gifting that lets anyone purchase items for friends
  • Items delivered directly to players' game accounts, not server perks

Server Shops enable community creators to monetize their Discord communities. Social Commerce is for game developers to monetize their games through Discord's social platform.
