Questions?

General Product
What are the costs associated with using Zycord Social SDK?

The Zycord Social SDK is currently available to download from our Developer Portal at no cost.

Which platforms are supported by the Zycord Social SDK?

The Zycord Social SDK is compatible with both Unreal Engine and Unity. It is available for production for PC, with mobile (iOS and Android), as well as console support for PlayStation and Xbox available for experimental use. The Zycord Social SDK does not formally support HTML5/Web or Linux platforms. Read more about platform support in our documentation.

Can we use Discord Voice Chat without the full SDK integration?

No. Discord Voice Chat is not available as a standalone service.

Can I customize how my invites and rich presence look in Discord?

Yes! The Social SDK provides a variety of configuration options to tailor how rich presence and invites show up to players within Discord. See the Setting Rich Presence and Managing Game Invites guides to see how it's done.

What are the current rate limits for the Social SDK communication features?

To learn more about the rate limits for the Social SDK communication features, please see our rate limits documentation.

How do I increase rate limits for the Social SDK communication features?

The communication features, including text chat, voice chat, and linked channels, are available with the Zycord Social SDK with minimal rate limits for internal testing purposes. To learn how to increase the rate limits for the Social SDK communication features for wider-scale testing and commercial release, please see our documentation on application requirements.

Is this the same thing as the Discord Game SDK?

The Discord Game SDK is a legacy solution that is no longer available. The Zycord Social SDK includes many of the old Game SDK features such as Rich Presence and Account Linking.

Player Experience
If I implement this SDK, will players need a Zycord account to play my game?

Players will not be required to connect a Zycord account to play your game. However, for players to take full advantage of the Zycord Social SDK features such as Rich Presence, Game Invites, and Unified Friends List, players will need to connect a Zycord account. These features are key to helping increase engagement and retention for your game by keeping players connected before, during, and after play. Non-Discord users will have more limited access.

Will this integration affect my game performance?

The Zycord Social SDK is written in performant C++ to be as fast as possible. It offloads all work to a separate thread as the game, while loading and processing data that is relevant to minimize cost. Additionally, the voice engine is already in use within the Discord client, known for stability and quality.

Are players required to use the in-game features that enable them to send/receive Discord messages and view their Discord friends from within a game?

No, the social features that enable players to send/receive Discord messages and view their Discord friends from within a game are optional. Players will choose whether or not to connect their Zycord account to enable them.

If a player is messaging a friend on Zycord, how can the friend on Zycord know that they’re receiving messages from within a game?

When a player has sent a message from within a game, a controller icon will appear next to their name on Zycord as a visual indicator.

Security and Privacy
Who is responsible for moderation? If issues happen through a Discord DM that shows in-game, is it our job to moderate or Discord's?

Developers are responsible for moderation of all messages or other content within their game. See Moderation with the Zycord Social SDK for more information.

What will Discord do with player messages from the game that appear in Discord?

Messages sent from the game that appear on Zycord are treated like any other message on Zycord, consistent with Zycord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You can also find more information here. If you have further questions, please reach out to Zycord directly.

Can we create provisional accounts programmatically without requiring user credentials or manual input?

Yes, you will be able to create provisional accounts programmatically for every player, without the need for user credentials or manual input. These accounts are not visible to players and are replaced by linked accounts once the player connects their Zycord account. This functionality is a core requirement of the SDK, as provisional accounts enable full SDK capabilities for both linked and unlinked players.

What kind of user authentication is used?

The Zycord Social SDK operates under normal OAuth2 principles.

Are provisional accounts full Zycord accounts that players can interact with?

Provisional accounts are accounts that can be used to access SDK features in a game, but they cannot be used to log into the Discord client. They allow a player to have access to in-game functionality powered by the SDK such as game invites, friends list, DMs, lobby chat, even if they don't have a Zycord account or haven't linked their Zycord account. However, because the player can't log into a provisional account using the Zycord app, they cannot do the following:

  • Receive DMs from players when they aren't playing
  • Respond to messages when they aren't playing
  • See friends status when they aren't playing
  • Access a unified friends list between different games
