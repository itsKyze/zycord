While we have no requirement to work from the office, we’re hiring most roles in the SF Bay Area to foster a vibrant central hub in the region. For most roles, candidates will need to reside in or be willing to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties).

Not every role requires candidates to be based in the SF Bay Area. In some cases, we continue to hire from across the U.S. and internationally. Your recruiter can share more about location expectations for the role you’re interested in.

We have our primary headquarters in San Francisco, California and an international hub in Amsterdam, Netherlands.

The majority of our roles are US-based. We are able to hire full-time employees in the US, Canada (Ontario and British Columbia), and the Netherlands. Other roles are engaged in international locations through our global Employer of Record partner.
